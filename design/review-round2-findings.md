# Design review round 2: findings (condensed)

## Player
P2-1 (must) Ch II–IV place far too few fights for their XP bands (Ch II ≈680 of 2,032; Ch III ≈1,300 of 4,001; Ch IV mostly skippable fights). → (a) scale enemyStats XP ~2.5× so ~8–10 placed fights per chapter meet the band, or (b) lower the bands; either way, list each chapter's placed fights with their XP sums.
P2-2 (should) Boss XP counts toward a band but arrives after the fight, so every boss is met 1–2 levels under the band. Azhrim (Lv 6 boss + brass serpent) is fought by Brannoc+Shevra alone at ~Lv 4–5 with no healer. Mother of Coils is fought by three right after the healer betrays. balance.mjs simulating Ch I bosses with the chapter-start snapshot (Brannoc Lv1) tests nothing. → Boss level = band top − 1; simulate with a pre-boss snapshot (real party at that point); tune Azhrim for two (stash items at the Moon Stair); in the Mother of Coils fight, have the Sea-Wolf crew throw draughts, or shorten it (Ptahk's channel ends at 50%).
P2-3 (should) Ch II has no dungeon; its main chain is a paperwork chain (key, flatter, signet, order, show order twice); the jailer's "right line" is undefined. → Merge the crew and ship steps; define the jailer's line; add one short dungeon (smugglers' tunnels under the Customs Warehouse as the crude route to the Brig, or a galley boarding fight on the Open Sea before the Storm).
P2-4 (should) Ch V finale has almost no puzzles; the founder's door answer is the always-held Starheart (a free click); twist 3 isn't used in a puzzle. → A time-loop puzzle planted in Ch IV: at the First Walls Kesh asks what to seal into the foundation stone / where to cut the founder's mark; in Ch V the Undercity door needs that item or answer (e.g. a Ch IV key item like the sling cord), with a fair clue (the mark itself; Shevra "you were there, Bear; what did you leave?").
P2-5 (should) Twist 2 is given away early (snake eyes at the Oasis is a near-reveal; too many blunt clues); the reveal doesn't explain why Tullia's blades and cultists ambushed and why Ptahk was there. → Make the Oasis moment ambiguous ("the smoke, my old eyes"); cut one or two blunt clues (Mitra hiss or cold touch); reveal speech pays off "Who do you think paid Tullia's blades? A rescuer is always trusted"; Tullia confirms in Ch V; at the choice Ptahk argues warmly for the Vault.
P2-6 (should) Zelara is a stand-in with no thread; the Vault branch has no story of its own. → Zelara's order guards the Standing Stones; Thoth's ancient cult burned her temple; in Ch V she finds Mitra's temple desecrated (scene); a line in each ending; in the Nine branch she still exists as an NPC at the camp who gives the Frost Flask; give the Vault branch one ending variant of its own.
P2-7 (nice) Explain the Gate route (Stones = the Tower's foundation; why the Watcher ended up in Shadizar) and why the statue's face reads clearly in Ch V (fire burned off centuries of soot; Shevra line).

## Art and sound director
A2-1 (must) Multi-part boss / group layouts overflow 320 px: pixelize `sprite` only caps height (width default h×1.6), so a dragon head can be 224 px wide and coils 160 px each; four `m` sprites up to 460 px. → Width caps per size passed as `--w`: s ≤ 64, m ≤ 80, l ≤ 100, xl ≤ 150; coils `--w 80`, head `--w 150` ("compact, coiled, head-on" in prompt). Battle-layout rule: slot centres for 1–4 enemies, total width ≤ 300, small overlap allowed.
A2-2 (should) No sfx for heal (most frequent action), steal, drink/thrown flask, summon, rumble/collapse, gate-open, Starheart heartbeat; writers can't invent ids. → Add `heal`, `steal`, `drink`, `summon`, `rumble`, `gate-open`, `heartbeat` (7 × $0.12) with synthesized fallbacks.
A2-3 (should) 48×48 face crop doesn't exist in pixelize.py; face position varies. → `face` mode with `--at x,y`; record crop points in ASSETS.md; portrait prompts "head and shoulders, face in the upper-centre third, head fills about half the frame" so default (24,8) usually works.
A2-4 (should) Scenes have ids but no content description; story-critical details may be missing (tower-crown chained Watcher with open chest; prison-nine Nine pinned under a pillar; statue holding a star; moon-stair mirrors + window south over the river; hoard weighing-altar + cracked dome; beach at night needs the ghost ship). statue-brannoc/statue-two don't pass the style anchor (and Nine) as references. → "Picture shows" column per picture, "no people unless named"; `bg-beach@night` → own scene or ghost ship as a scene card; pass references for the statue set.
A2-5 (should) Fixed-grid icon sheet cutting is fragile; 4×5 on a square canvas gives non-square cells; "19" vs 20 listed; all ten consumables share one potion icon. → Chroma-key background + wide gutters, cut by connected components with a count check (fixed grid as fallback); sheet 1 as 5×4; consumable code tints (potion@red/blue/green/gold, fire-flask potion@orange…).
A2-6 (should) Which file is passed as reference (full-res candidate vs pixelized)? Enemy prompts lack the "style only" clause. → Keep full-res anchors in `.gamegen/anchors/` and pass those; add "Reference image is for style only (palette, outline weight, shading, pixel rendering); do not copy its subject, pose or clothing." to every enemy and portrait prompt.
A2-7 (should) Id gaps: Ygg-Sothar speaks but has no NPC/portrait; Ch III quartermaster and Kesh's grandsons missing; cut 5 breaks c5-cellar; `azhrim` is both a portrait and sprite id. → NPC `ygg-sothar` (portrait cropped from the head sprite in code, or generic); `quartermaster`, `kesh-grandson` generic; cut 5 also remaps c5-cellar; rename sprite `azhrim-fight`.
A2-8 (nice) mp3 ambience loops click (encoder padding) → trim silence / self-crossfade 0.3 s; boss "scale pulse" breaks the pixel grid → 1 px bob; make ASSETS.md and the design agree on the price source.
