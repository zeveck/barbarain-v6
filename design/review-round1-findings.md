# Design review round 1: findings (condensed, all three reviewers)

## Player
P1 (must) Arena Rank 3 (Lv 6+) and Ch II/III side quests can be missed with no warning; chapters are one-way. → Unfinished Pit ranks carry over to Ch V; Rank 3 Lv 5+; "point of no return" confirmation before each chapter exit listing open quests, with "Not yet".
P2 (should) Seeing Ending C after taking the Vault needs replaying ~1 hour; autosave overwrites. Choice is lopsided (most will free Nine). → Protected "Before the Choice" save; after first ending unlock replay from the choice; make the Vault side more tempting (Kingsbane + Ilsa's crew help in a Ch V battle, not just ending text).
P3 (should) Battles cost ~30–60 taps; no auto/repeat/speed. → Auto button (attack weakest using known weakness), Repeat last round, battle speed setting.
P4 (should) Clever puzzle solutions skip fights → less XP → Ch I boss harder, no healer yet. → Solved puzzles give at least the XP of the fight they replace ("+XP: clever!"); a draught stash/rest point before the Tower Crown.
P5 (should) Phone portrait leaves ~150px for the text log. → Thin HP-pip party row while exploring (tap to expand); picture can collapse to a banner; full strip only in battle; test 390×844 and 360×740.
P6 (should) Moon Stair clue unfair: nothing tells the player the river is south, throne north. → Put directions in room text (window looks south over the river; mirror 3 Look mentions the King's palace to the north), or Shevra comments at the painting.
P7 (should) Starheart driven into the dragon twice (Ch IV boss and Ending A) — a rerun. → Different Ch IV finishing move (collapse the hoard ceiling / nomads cast down the mountain; heart only stuns it), or change Ending A.
P8 (should) Exploration needs "new" markers and faster item use (4 taps). → Dot on thing chips/exits with something new or unvisited; Use lists key items first, newest at top; after a failed use, one-tap "Try another item".
P9 (nice) Weapon swap unclear (from whole bag?); weakness text contradicts itself (iron dead lightning). → Second "sidearm" slot that Swap toggles, shown on the command button; clean weakness table; give Nine a Blunt/Star option vs iron enemies.

## Builder
B1 (must) No shared manifest of cross-chapter ids/flags/NPCs/assets/chapter-start states; ~67 rooms vs 47 scenes, many rooms without a scene (Ape Rocks, Serpent River, Power Wells, Prison of Nine, Hall of Echoes, Molten Bridge, Ch V Jackal/Pit/Temple); unsourced items (glow-fish oil, red ochre, Kraken rum for both jailer and Valerio, frost-gourd). → Core builder writes content/manifest.js: every cross-chapter item/flag/NPC/asset id, room→bg table (with tinted variants like `bg-tavern@night`), chapter-start snapshots per chapter and branch (Ch IV/V Nine vs Zelara), id prefixes per chapter; helpers use manifest ids only.
B2 (must) Star-cell count contradictory (door needs two; "you have three"; Starheart one of them would mean the door can't open before the betrayal). → Pick one rule: e.g. Gate has three sockets (two cells + the Starheart); a separate third cell found in the Prison of Nine goes to Nine or the Vault. Gate the choice behind having both Gate cells.
B3 (must) "No dead ends" unchecked; snow-melon used for both bridge and fake heart. → tools/lint-content.mjs (Node): referenced ids exist, rooms reachable, consumable key items have a repeatable source or single use, every condition can become true. Hermit always has another melon.
B4 (must) Effect runner's hard parts undesigned: async awaits for fight/dialogue/choice; what retry restores (double rewards?); autosave before entry scripts; mid-battle triggers (half HP); reserved snapshot for "before the final choice". → Specify in CONTENT_SCHEMA: async run; defeat aborts script and restores pre-script snapshot; `once` blocks; battle `triggers`/phases; reserved slot.
B5 (must) One recorded solution per chapter misses branches; RNG battles make replays brittle; how buttons are named unclear. → Stable `data-act` attributes; fixed seed per solution; scripted battle policy "auto"; solution files: ch1, ch2, ch3-free, ch3-vault, ch4-nine, ch4-zelara, ch5-A/B/C, sq-arena, sq-witch, sq-bride; one full New Game→ending run with real input.
B6 (should) No balance numbers across 5 writers. → Party growth tables; `enemyStats(level, role)` (minion, brute, caster, boss) writers call; gear power curve per chapter; tools/balance.mjs sums XP per chapter vs bands and simulates bosses. Remove the self-correcting weakness sentence.
B7 (should) Audio: plan unknown (free plan would be 64 kbps, credit line, ~10k credits — too few); music has no seamless loop. → First generation one sfx at default format to settle plan; generate in priority order (title, battle, shadizar, boss, stings…); synthesized Web Audio fallback for every sfx; loop music by crossfading into itself at the loop point.
B8 (should) Pixel pipeline: box filter makes mush; soft alpha halos; Pillow median-cut quantize doesn't accept RGBA; ~1.22× scale on phones uneven; per-image palettes differ. → tools/pixelize.py once, tested on the anchor: LANCZOS to 2× then nearest to 1× (or grid detect); alpha threshold ~128 + edge bleed; quantize everything against one shared master palette with alpha handled separately. Display at largest integer scale, or pre-upscale 4× and draw with smoothing for even pixels.
B9 (should) Budget no retry margin except scenes; scenes overpriced (nanogen flash 1K ≈ $0.034, 512 ≈ $0.022). → Scenes at flash 1K; icons as grid sheets; tinted variant enemies from the start; look up OpenAI pricing; ~15% reserve.
B10 (should) Phone layout has no height budget; test sizes don't match the playtest guide. → Height budget per screen; thin party row outside battle; picture ≤ ~35% screen height on short phones; dock one tab at a time in ≤ two rows; add 375×667, 852×393, 1280×720, 800×600 to definition of done.

## Art and sound director
A1 (must) Budget has no margin; nanogen defaults to `pro` 2K for scenes ($0.134) unless `--model flash` is passed; imagegen2 quality/size not pinned; no retries on audio. → Pin nanogen `--model flash --size 1K`; imagegen2 `--quality low|medium`; icon sheets; look up OpenAI price; ≥$3 contingency.
A2 (must) No single shared palette; per-image adaptive quantize; transparent edge halos. → One master palette file (`game/assets/palette.png`, 48 colours of ramps from the 7 key hues + skin and sea tones); quantize everything against it (dither on scenes, off for sprites/icons); downscale, threshold alpha to 1 bit, add 1px Ink outline; pipeline recorded as a script.
A3 (should) Brannoc bust as the only reference for 35 enemies will leak human torsos into monsters; street scene as reference for desert/storm/void scenes. → A second, full-body sprite anchor (a Set cultist) for enemies; Brannoc bust only for portraits; nanogen scenes pass the street anchor with "use only palette, lighting and pixel rendering; new composition"; one approach for scene style; test three very different scenes before the batch.
A4 (must) Multi-part bosses have no part sprites; bosses up to 170px leave no room. → `dragon-final-head`, `dragon-final-coil` (mirrored in code), Ch IV equivalents as separate sprites; fire/void phases as code tint; cap bosses ~140px; layout note: head centred, coils at x≈60 and x≈260, bottom-aligned y≈170.
A5 (should) Big story moments without pictures: Prison of Nine (the choice), Serpent River, Ape Rocks, Bone Fields, Ch V Tower Ruins; statue face payoff unreadable at 320×180 in a wide square; prologue and chapter cards; founding scene. → bg-prison-nine; statue close-up set made by editing one base (worn, Brannoc's face, serpent face, two figures); bg-founding; map prologue/cards to scenes; room→picture table with code tints.
A6 (should) Puzzle items all share one "key item" icon; the Starheart has no image. → ~25 key-item icons as 2–3 grid sheets; a dedicated 64×64 Starheart sprite (pulse + app icon).
A7 (should) Non-integer pixel scaling (96→64 portrait in strip; scene fit; battle zoom). → 48×48 face crop for the party strip or 96 at 0.5×; scenes at largest integer scale framed by the border, fractional only above 3×; battle darkens without zoom.
A8 (should) SFX missing: cold spell, status tick, buff, debuff, defend, bite, refusal thud, save chime, generic monster roar; no ambience loops (sea, storm, crowd, fire, jungle/marsh). → Add those (~14 × $0.12).
A9 (should) No music area map; same boss track for all; Ending B uses the noble theme; no loop seam plan; stings as music clips. → Room/chapter→track table; dark variation for Ending B (or reuse "ancient"); crossfade each track into itself ~2s at the loop point; stings via `audiogen sfx`; title theme first as reference.
